# Youth Math Research Collection — future project (not yet started)

**The idea (user note-to-self, 2026-10-13).** Compile a collection of mathematics research work done by people at the undergraduate level and below, organized/divided by the main mathematics disciplines. To be taken up at a later time, AFTER the CGT paper-pattern survey project (see the `cgt-paper-survey` topic) concludes.

**Scope notes carried over from the survey's Batch N experience (the seed of this idea):**
- ACCEPTED papers only — peer-reviewed venues with pinned citations; never proposals, preprints without acceptance, or mere surveys (the survey's binding verification policy, in its SOURCES.md section 3, is the natural template).
- Web-verified metadata; author level (high school / middle school / undergraduate / amateur) recorded honestly, including mentor-coauthor caveats and any graduate-student coauthors — the survey's honest-notes discipline (e.g., N2/N8 include the faculty mentor; N3 a graduate student).
- Organized by main math disciplines (the survey's Batch N was combinatorics-only: N1 Jackson-Johnson, N2/N8 Sah-Sawhney-Stoner-Zhao, N3 Jain-Sah-Sawhney, N4 PRIMES STEP chip-firing, N5/N6 the PRIMES STEP stable-matching pair, N7 Kuszmaul — 8 rows, all acceptance-verified, the combinatorics seed of this collection).
- Useful known venues/programs to scout: PRIMES / PRIMES STEP / PRIMES-USA (MIT), RSI, the Morgan Prize (AMS-MAA-SIAM) record and its honorable mentions, college/research journals that publish pre-doctoral work (Amer. Math. Monthly, College Math. J., J. Integer Seq., Electron. J. Combin., Enumer. Comb. Appl., Advances in Combinatorics, Journal of Algebra candidate pending acceptance verification).
- Standing rule from the survey: entries are never added silently; exclusion/acceptance decisions are dated and logged.

**Status:** idea only — not started. First step when taken up: write the project charter + register (mirroring the survey's SOURCES.md pattern), then seed the disciplines.
