# I2 - Erdos, Ko, Rado (1961): Intersection theorems for systems of finite sets

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): P. Erdos, Chao Ko, R. Rado, "Intersection theorems for systems of finite sets", Quart. J. Math. Oxford 12(1): 313-320 (1961), DOI 10.1093/qmath/12.1.313. Open PDF at the Renyi Institute: https://www.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1961-07.pdf. The 1938/1961 publication story verified via the historical survey arXiv:0808.0774.
- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the founding theorem of extremal intersecting-set theory: for n >= 2k, a family of k-subsets of an n-set in which any two members intersect has size at most C(n-1, k-1), with equality for a star; the paper also proves the t-intersecting analogue with its threshold and star-type extremal families.
- Decisive moves: (1) [P8] extremal optimization: the star is forced as the extremal structure among intersecting families; (2) [P4] the paper's own engine is a delicate induction on the ground set with structural case analysis - this is what actually proves the bound; (3) [P20 MISS - honest] the expected weight-shifting/symmetrization engine does NOT appear in the original: shifting proofs (later, via the Frankl program) and Katona's circle method are retrospective engines, recorded as an honest miss for the Atlas's silent P20.
