- Summary (flagged sketch, the algebraic-graph canon): the Moore-graph classification's origin. E. F. Moore posed the problem of describing the graphs of given degree and diameter with maximum order (the Moore bound); the extremal cases are the Moore graphs (equivalently, regular graphs of girth twice the diameter plus one). The theorem: a Moore graph of diameter 2 has degree 2 (the pentagon), 3 (the Petersen graph), 7 (the Hoffman-Singleton graph on 50 vertices - constructed in the paper), or possibly 57 (3250 vertices - still open, the field's long-standing problem); a Moore graph of diameter 3 exists only for degree 2 (the 7-cycle).
