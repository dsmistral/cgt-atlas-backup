- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): Berge's strong perfect graph conjecture, resolved: every Berge graph (no odd hole, no odd antihole) either belongs to one of four basic classes (bipartite graphs, their complements, line graphs of bipartite graphs, their complements) or admits a 2-join or skew-partition decomposition.
