# B6 - Park-Pham (2022): A proof of the Kahn-Kalai conjecture

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): J. Park, H. T. Pham, arXiv:2203.17207 (31 March 2022; v2 April 2023); Journal of the American Mathematical Society, published electronically August 7, 2023 (Wikipedia lists the print year as 2024). Sources: arXiv abstract; JAMS copy (par.nsf.gov); Wikipedia Kahn-Kalai conjecture; IAS and Stanford announcements.
- Summary (arXiv abstract, quoted-level): proves the expectation-threshold conjecture of Kahn and Kalai - for any increasing property F on a finite set X, p_c(F) = O(q(F) log l(F)), where p_c is the threshold, q the expectation threshold, and l(F) the maximum size of a minimal member of F. The proof also settles a conjecture of Talagrand on selector processes. The preprint is famously short (8 KB).
- Decisive moves: (1) [CAND-A3] threshold/window - the conjecture proved; (2) the spread measure - the argument inducts on the spread of distributions; a weaker fractional version was proved earlier by Frankston-Kahn-Narayanan-Park (Annals). Recorded as a watch-item (single firing in corpus).
