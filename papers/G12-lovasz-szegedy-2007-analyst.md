# G12 - Lovasz-Szegedy (2007): Szemeredi's lemma for the analyst

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): L. Lovasz and B. Szegedy, "Szemeredi's lemma for the analyst", GAFA Geom. Funct. Anal. 17: 252-270 (2007), DOI 10.1007/s00039-007-0599-6. Sources: SpringerLink (GAFA 17, 252-270).
- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the regularity lemma restated as analysis: every bounded measurable function on [0,1]^2 is close in cut norm to a step function with a bounded number of steps. The reformulation launched the graph limits program - the limit object (graphon) becomes the natural home of the regularity lemma.
