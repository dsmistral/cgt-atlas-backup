# F3 - Erdos-Szekeres (1935): A combinatorial problem in geometry

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): P. Erdos, G. Szekeres, "A combinatorial problem in geometry", Compositio Mathematica 2 (1935): 463-470. OPEN PDF located: numdam.org (CM_1935__2__463_0). Sources: numdam; EUDML.
- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the Happy Ending problem - every sufficiently large point set in general position contains k points in convex position. The paper proves ES(k) <= C(2k-2, k-1) by the cups-caps induction, proves the Erdos-Szekeres monotone-subsequence lemma on the way, and the same argument yields the classical diagonal Ramsey upper bound r(k,k) <= C(2k-2, k-1).
- Decisive moves: (1) [P9] the ES lemma - among (r-1)(s-1)+1 distinct numbers there is a monotone subsequence of length r or s; pigeonhole on (length, endpoint) pairs; (2) [P8] the extremal reading - take the longest monotone subsequence; its structure forces the partition; (3) [P4] the cups-caps induction on k giving the binomial bound; (4) cross-domain note: the same binomial bound is the Erdos-Szekeres Ramsey upper bound - the famous "Szekeres lemma" story (Erdos credited the whole 1935 breakthrough to the lemma).
