# J2 - Lovasz (1979): On the Shannon capacity of a graph

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): L. Lovasz, "On the Shannon capacity of a graph", IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory 25(1): 1-7 (1979), DOI 10.1109/TIT.1979.1055985. - Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): introduces the theta function theta(G) via orthonormal representations of the graph, proves the sandwich alpha(G) <= theta(G) <= chi(complement of G), proves multiplicativity theta(G x H) = theta(G)theta(H) for the strong product (hence the Shannon capacity Theta(G) <= theta(G)), and computes Theta(C5) = sqrt(5) - solving the pentagon capacity problem open since Shannon 1956.
- Decisive moves: (1) [P21 HIT] the engine is spectral/geometric: an orthonormal-representation optimization whose value is pinned by an eigenvalue-type program; the famous umbrella construction gives theta(C5) = sqrt(5); (2) [P37 secondary - synthesis call] the paper works with a max/min pair of equivalent characterizations of theta - an early primal-dual optimization argument in combinatorics; recorded as a widening of the P37 family note (E4 planar duality, H10 Konig minimax, I11 LP duality, now optimization duality) - synthesis decides whether to credit it as a family firing; (3) [P19 MISS - honest] the seed expected rank/linear algebra, but theta is an optimization quantity, not a rank/dimension bound - and the honest twist is that the rank answer arrives in the very next paper of this batch: Haemers' minrank (J4), introduced partly in response to questions posed in this paper. The J2-J4 pair is the survey's cleanest same-problem engine contrast since H6 vs H7.
