## 1. P43+ verdicts (the load-bearing test applied)

### PROPOSED P43: semi-random nibble construction with parameter tracking (CAND-A2)

The survey's strongest load-bearing case. Fires across FIVE batches, with a complete origin-formalization-application arc:
- A6 (Ajtai-Komlos-Szemeredi 1981) and A8 (Kim 1990, R(3,t)) - the ancestors;
- B4 (Kahn) - the guided-random engine, named in Kahn's own abstract;
- L6 (Rodl 1985) - the ORIGIN: the nibble is born proving the Erdos-Hanani conjecture, and the candidate's name descends literally from the method;
- L9 (Pippenger-Spencer 1989) - the FORMALIZATION: the explicit parameter-tracking framework, the canonical nibble lemma;
- L8 (Keevash 2014) - the APPLICATION: the nibble as a workhorse inside the designs existence proof.

Shape: build the object by a controlled adaptive random process, tracking densities via potential functions / differential equations, then alter. Distinct from P12 (the process is adaptive) and from P14 (the pruning is guided by the tracked parameters).

### PROPOSED P44: threshold/window (CAND-A3)

Fires across four batches: A2/A3 (Erdos-Renyi - the origin instances), B3 (Friedgut-Kalai - sharp thresholds), B5 (Kahn-Kalai - the expectation threshold conjecture), B6 (Park-Pham - the proof). The synthesis headline: THE WINDOW IS THE THEOREM - the pattern's content is not a proof technique but a phenomenon that every paper in this line calibrates to. A phenomenon-pattern in the manner of P26, and the survey recommends it on the same grounds.

### NOT promoted: CAND-A1 - the Lovasz Local Lemma

The B9 presentation-level flag is resolved AGAINST: the LLL step credited to Johansson lives in the Molloy-Reed expositions, not in Johansson's own DIMACS Technical Report 91-5 (itself never journal-published). By the letter of the load-bearing test the evidence is single-batch (A5/A7/A9). External-canon recommendation (section 8): the Atlas session should either name the LLL as its own entry or record it as P13's sparse-dependency strengthening.

### DIES in-corpus: CAND-A4 - entropy compression

The gate was never crossed: no firing in D/E/F/G/H, none in the M watch (13a-13c). The external lineage is recorded for honesty (Molloy 2019 sharpening Johansson by the entropy-compression method), with P29 as the counting parent. The method is real field practice; this corpus never sampled a genuine firing of it.

### DEMOTED: CAND-B5 - hypergraph containers

B7 (Balogh-Morris-Samotij) and B8 (Saxton-Thomason) are independent discoveries with multi-domain recurrence inside, but they sit in a single batch; the letter of the two-batch gate governs. External-evidence recommendation (section 8): the container method is a modern field workhorse and deserves an Atlas home on external evidence, not on this corpus.

### DEMOTED: CAND-C1 - analytic combinatorics

Three firings (C2 Otter, C6, C3) - all inside Batch C; the cross-batch test cannot be satisfied in-corpus. External-canon recommendation to the Atlas session (Flajolet-Sedgewick); P5 is the generating-function parent.

### NOT a separate pattern: CAND-G1 - transference / dense model

The transference move is the FACE of P30 (regularity) and P31 (removal): how the regularity/removal engine deploys on relative versions (G11 Green-Tao the flagship; G9 the relative-removal lineage).
