# F8 - Bergelson-Leibman (1996): Polynomial extensions of van der Waerden's and Szemeredi's theorems

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): V. Bergelson, A. Leibman, "Polynomial extensions of van der Waerden's and Szemeredi's theorems", Journal of the American Mathematical Society 9(3) (July 1996): 725-753, DOI 10.1090/S0894-0347-96-00194-4, MR 1325795. OPEN PDF located: ams.org (journal page). Sources: AMS PDF; Semantic Scholar abstract; MathSciNet.
- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the polynomial Szemeredi theorem: a positive-density subset of Z^l contains configurations {u + p_i(n) v_i} for arbitrary integer-valued polynomials p_i with p_i(0) = 0 (e.g., {x, x+n, x+2n^2}); proved as a multiple-recurrence statement for commuting operators with polynomial exponents, the PET (polynomial ergodic theorem) induction at its heart.
- Decisive moves: (1) [P4] the PET induction - an elaborate induction on systems of polynomial expressions, organized by weight matrices (visible in the open PDF): the paper's technical core and a genuinely new face of P4; (2) [P25] the ergodic transference - the combinatorial statement is carried into topological dynamics (multiple recurrence), where the induction lives, and translated back; (3) cross-domain note: the founding paper of polynomial Ramsey theory.
