- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the regularity lemma and its counting lemma generalized to k-uniform hypergraphs, with quasirandomness tested on octahedral patterns and decompositions sensitive to relative density; as the application, the first purely combinatorial proof of the multidimensional Szemeredi theorem of Furstenberg-Katznelson.
