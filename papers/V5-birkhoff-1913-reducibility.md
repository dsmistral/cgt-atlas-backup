- Summary (flagged sketch, the four-colour histories): reducibility is born. Birkhoff defines a configuration of countries as REDUCIBLE if no minimal counterexample to the four-colour theorem can contain it, and proves rings of countries reducible by analysing the ways a ring's interior connects to the ring's colourings - the "Birkhoff diamond" (the order-4 ring) the canonical configuration of the theory. The method: count the colourings of the ring map (the chromatic-polynomial arithmetic - Birkhoff's own 1912 predecessor, "A determinant formula for the number of ways of coloring a map", Ann. of Math. 14 (1912) 42-46, uncorpus'd lineage; the chromatic polynomial's birth) and show the interior extensions are too few to sustain a minimal counterexample. Kempe interchanges appear inside the ring-colouring analysis as the engine for permuting and freeing colours - the 1879 device now working inside a valid program.
