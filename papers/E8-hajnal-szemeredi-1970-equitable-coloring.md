- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the Hajnal-Szemeredi theorem - every graph of maximum degree Delta has an equitable coloring with Delta + 1 colors (equitable = color-class sizes differ by at most one). The proof takes a minimal counterexample with a maximal partial equitable coloring and moves vertices along auxiliary alternating chains of color classes until an uncolored vertex finds a slot.
- Decisive moves: (1) [P15] minimal counterexample + maximal partial coloring setup; (2) [P16] migration chains - the uncolored vertex is inserted by cascading moves along an auxiliary digraph of color classes, an augmentation move for colorings (a new face of P16, matching-style alternating paths applied to class sizes); (3) [P20-flavored] weight balancing - the cascade keeps class sizes balanced; recorded as P20-ADJACENT only (weak evidence; the real P20 test is Katona, Batch K); (4) [P18] lineage: Kierstead-Kostochka produced polynomial-time algorithms for equitable colorings from the proof.
