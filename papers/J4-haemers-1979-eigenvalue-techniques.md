- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the thesis deploys eigenvalue and rank techniques across design theory and graph theory; its most famous deliverable is the minrank bound on Shannon capacity: if the complement of G admits a fitting matrix of rank r (over any field), then Theta(G) <= r - a bound incomparable with the Lovasz theta function (each beats the other on some graphs), introduced partly to answer three questions Lovasz posed in J2.
