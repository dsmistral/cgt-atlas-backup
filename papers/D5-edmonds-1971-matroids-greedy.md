# D5 - Edmonds (1971): Matroids and the greedy algorithm

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): J. Edmonds, "Matroids and the greedy algorithm", Mathematical Programming 1 (1971): 127-136, DOI 10.1007/BF01584082. Sources: Springer; ACM DL; standard reference listings.
- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the characterization theorem - greedy (sort by weight, add feasible elements) is optimal for every weight function iff the feasible sets are the independent sets of a matroid; plus the polyhedral view - matroid rank as the solution of a linear program over the matroid polyhedron.
- Decisive moves: (1) [P18] the greedy procedure itself; (2) [P4] the exchange/induction argument proving both directions - the exchange axiom is exactly what greedy's swaps need; (3) [P17] the polyhedral certificate - rank as an LP over a polyhedron, a min-max/duality reading of greedy optimality; (4) [P25] reduction as classification - optimization problems sorted by the encoding of their feasible sets (which structures make greedy work).
