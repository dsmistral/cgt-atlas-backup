# A9 - Moser-Tardos (2010): A constructive proof of the general Lovasz Local Lemma

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): R. A. Moser, G. Tardos, J. ACM 57(2), Article 11, 2010; arXiv:0903.0544 (open). Sources: arXiv abstract page; ACM DL listing; Wikipedia (Godel Prize 2020 for the algorithmic LLL).
- Summary (publisher abstract, quoted-level): the LLL, discovered by Erdos and Lovasz in 1975, non-constructively proves existence of combinatorial objects meeting prescribed criteria; this paper gives a randomized algorithm - repeatedly resample the violated events - that finds such an object in expected polynomial time under the same general LLL preconditions, no stronger assumptions.
- Decisive moves: (1) [P18] the proof IS the procedure: termination analysis of the resampling algorithm replaces the existential argument; (2) [CAND-A4] entropy compression - the witness-tree log-size argument: if the algorithm ran long, the execution log would compress random bits below their entropy, a contradiction; (3) [CAND-A1] constructive version of the general asymmetric LLL.
