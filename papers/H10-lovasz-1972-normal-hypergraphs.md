- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): resolution of the (weak) perfect graph conjecture - complements of perfect graphs are perfect - via normal hypergraphs: a hypergraph is normal iff the maximum number of disjoint hyperedges coincides with the minimum number of vertices representing the hyperedges in every partial hypergraph (verified from the paper's record). The graph problem is recast as a hypergraph minimax property, connecting perfection to Konig-type theorems.
