# D1 - Kuhn (1955): The Hungarian method for the assignment problem

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): H. W. Kuhn, "The Hungarian method for the assignment problem", Naval Research Logistics Quarterly 2(1-2): 83-97 (1955), DOI 10.1002/nav.3800020109. Sources: Wiley Online Library; RePEc listing.
- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the assignment problem - n persons to n jobs, maximize total score - solved by a finite method exploiting ideas latent in the work of two Hungarian mathematicians (Konig and Egervary): the method maintains row/column potentials (a dual solution) and improves a partial assignment by alternating-path adjustments until potentials and assignment complement each other.
- Decisive moves: (1) [P17] min-max duality as the engine - feasible potentials versus attainable assignment value, with optimality certified when the two meet (Konig's theorem in modern terms); (2) [P16] alternating-path augmentation - a partial assignment improves along alternating paths between exposed rows and columns; (3) [P25] the assignment problem encoded in the bipartite matching/potential language where the min-max theorem lives; (4) [P18] the proof is a procedure - termination proves optimality.
