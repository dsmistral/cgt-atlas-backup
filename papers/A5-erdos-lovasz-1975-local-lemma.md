# A5 - Erdos-Lovasz (1975): Problems and results on 3-chromatic hypergraphs and some related questions

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): P. Erdos, L. Lovasz, in: Infinite and Finite Sets (to Paul Erdos on his 60th birthday), Colloq. Math. Soc. Janos Bolyai 10, North-Holland, pp. 609-627, 1975. Sources: Springer LLL survey chapter citation; Wikipedia Lovasz local lemma (weaker version proved there, 4pd <= 1; Lemma I dated 1973, published 1975).
- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the debut of the Lovasz Local Lemma - amid results on property B and 3-chromatic uniform hypergraphs, the paper proves that if each of many mostly-independent bad events has probability at most p and each event depends on at most d others, then 4pd <= 1 implies a positive probability that no bad event occurs. The proof is an induction on the dependency graph.
- Decisive moves: (1) [CAND-A1] the local lemma itself - unlike [P13]'s sum-below-1 condition, it exploits near-independence to tolerate probabilities far above 1/(number of events); (2) [P4] the induction on the dependency graph that proves it; (3) [P13] union bound appears as the degenerate d=0 special case.
- New-pattern candidates: CAND-A1 (Lovasz Local Lemma) - leading P43 candidate of the whole survey: no P-ID in P1-P42 names it (P13 covers only the union bound), yet it fires here (debut), in A7's lineage (Shearer's optimal threshold), in A9 (algorithmization), and in countless later papers to be verified in Batches B and L.
