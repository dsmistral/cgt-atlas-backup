- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the origin of the stability method: an H-free graph with ex(n,H) edges - or within o(n^2) of the extremum - must be structurally close to the Turan graph T_{chi(H)-1}(n). Combined with an induction squeezing the remaining structural freedom, this became the standard template for exact extremal results and, decades later, the backbone of the modern stability approach to combinatorial problems.
