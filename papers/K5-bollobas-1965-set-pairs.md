# K5 - Bollobas (1965): On generalized graphs

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): B. Bollobas, "On generalized graphs", Acta Math. Acad. Sci. Hungar. 16: 447-452 (1965), DOI 10.1007/BF01904851. Sources: Springer article page (confirms the citation); University of Memphis digital commons record.
- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the two-families theorem (the Bollobas set-pairs inequality): if pairs (A_i, B_i) satisfy |A_i| = a, |B_i| = b, and A_i meets B_j exactly when i = j, then the number of pairs is at most C(a+b, a) - sharp. The inequality is the grandfather of the LYM-type extension program (skew-Bollobas, Frankl's inhomogeneous forms).
- Decisive moves: (1) [P9 HIT] the engine is permutation double counting: for a random ordering of the ground set, the events "all of A_i precedes all of B_i" are pairwise disjoint, and each occurs with probability 1/C(a+b, a) - disjoint positive-probability events sum to at most 1; (2) note: the argument is the LYM-permutation engine's sibling (compare K7's circle method and the LYM proof's retrospective role at I1) - the same averaging-over-orderings family.
