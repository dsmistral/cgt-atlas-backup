- Abstract or faithful summary (summarized, flagged sketch): given generators for a permutation group G on a colored set, the COLOR AUTOMORPHISM problem asks for generators of the subgroup of G stabilizing the color classes. Main theorem: testing isomorphism of graphs of valence at most t is polynomial-time reducible to the color automorphism problem for groups whose composition factors have bounded order ("small simple sections"). Divide-and-conquer is then used on the group, trading the problem on G for a small number of similar problems on a smaller group; the trivalent case is elementary (P = G), and higher valence requires new observations about primitive permutation groups. The 1980 FOCS abstract records the conference-abstract priority.
