# E9 - Thomassen (1994): Every planar graph is 5-choosable

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): C. Thomassen, "Every planar graph is 5-choosable", Journal of Combinatorial Theory Series B 62 (1994): 180-181 - pages per standard citation; DOI 10.1006/jctb.1994.1062. Sources: ACM DL; ScienceDirect record.
- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): a two-page proof that every planar graph is 5-choosable, resolving the first of the two Erdos-Rubin-Taylor planar conjectures. The proof is an induction on vertices with a strengthened hypothesis: prove instead the statement for plane graphs whose outer cycle carries list sizes 3 (two adjacent vertices) and 5, so that a chordless outer cycle can be consumed from a vertex with an unlisted neighbor.
- Decisive moves: (1) [P15] the strengthened induction hypothesis - proving a stronger statement (outerplanar boundary lists) so the induction closes; the flagship modern instance of this meta-move; (2) [P8] the chord structure argument - whether the outer cycle has a chord dictates the decomposition; (3) honest contrast: the proof needs NO discharging - a standing counterpoint to P36-style arguments in planar coloring (recorded for the synthesis's face inventory); (4) [Kempe-watch] chain-flavored list recoloring at the induction step.
