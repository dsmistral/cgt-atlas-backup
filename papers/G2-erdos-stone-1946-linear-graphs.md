- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the theorem that settled the chromatic case of extremal graph theory: a graph with more than (1 - 1/(r-1)) n^2/2 + c n^{2-1/t} edges contains a small blow-up K_r(t) of the complete r-chromatic graph. With Erdos's 1962 quantitative sharpening this becomes the limit theorem recorded at G3. The bipartite (degenerate) case is left open and remains so.
