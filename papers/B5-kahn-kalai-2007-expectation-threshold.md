# B5 - Kahn-Kalai (2006/2007): Thresholds and expectation thresholds

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): J. Kahn, G. Kalai, arXiv:math/0603218 (March 2006); journal version: Combinatorics, Probability and Computing 16: 495-502 (2007). TITLE CORRECTED from the seed ("A threshold problem for hypergraphs" is not the title; it is "Thresholds and expectation thresholds"). Sources: arXiv abstract; Kahn publication list (CPC 16 (2007), 495-502); Wikipedia Kahn-Kalai conjecture.
- Summary (arXiv abstract, quoted-level): for a random graph in G(n,p) and the property of containing a copy of H, let q be the threshold for expected copies of every subgraph H' of H, and p the threshold for actual containment; CONJECTURE: p/q = O(log n), with related conjectures for general Boolean functions and a possible connection to discrete isoperimetry.
- Decisive moves: (1) [CAND-A3] threshold/window - the expectation threshold conjecture is the quantitative form of the gap between naive expectation counting and the true threshold; (2) [P10] the expectation threshold q is itself an averaging/expectation bound - the naive lower bound the conjecture says is nearly tight.
