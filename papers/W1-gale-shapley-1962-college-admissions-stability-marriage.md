- Summary (flagged sketch, the stable-matching canon): the stable matching existence theorem's origin. An assignment is unstable if a man and a woman each prefer the other to their assigned partners (a blocking pair). The deferred-acceptance procedure: each man proposes down his preference list; each woman defers her best received proposal and rejects the rest; no man proposes twice to the same woman; the procedure terminates (at most n^2 proposals) and yields a stable matching - the rejection argument: a blocking pair would require a woman to have rejected a man she preferred, or a man never to have reached her. The paper proves the outcome optimal for all men simultaneously (no man does better in any stable matching) - the lattice-optimality theory's birth; the college-admissions variant (quotas, many-to-one) carried in the same paper; the strategic-manipulation results and the market-design clearinghouses the later life (the Roth account; centralized deferred-acceptance mechanisms predate the theory).
