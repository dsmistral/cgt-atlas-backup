- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the founding document of minor thinking: Hadwiger proposes classifying graphs ("Streckenkomplexe") by contractions, in a form aimed at the chromatic number - every loopless graph NOT contractible onto the complete graph K_{t+1} is t-colourable; equivalently, every (t+1)-chromatic graph is contractible onto K_{t+1}. The small cases are proved in the paper (t <= 4 per the standard account); t = 5 is equivalent to the four-colour theorem (Wagner's later work).
- Decisive moves: (1) [P34] contraction as the structural move - literally the first organized use of the contraction/minor viewpoint, decades before graph-minor theory; (2) the conjecture itself is the founding bridge between coloring and minor structure - the program P34 serves in the modern literature (Hadwiger's conjecture, Kostochka-Thomason degree bounds, Robertson-Seymour); (3) reducibility-style case analysis for the small t.
