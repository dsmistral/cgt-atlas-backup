- Abstract or faithful summary (summarized, flagged sketch): proves the Existence Conjecture: for fixed t and k, the Steiner system S(t,k,n) exists for all sufficiently large n satisfying the necessary divisibility conditions - divisibility is the whole story for lambda=1 and all t, the exact Keevash analogue of Wilson's theorem (L4, the t=2 PBD case). The proof combines THREE engines: (1) the RANDOMIZED ALGEBRAIC CONSTRUCTION - a "template" built by algebraic methods over finite fields (the image of a random linear map), carrying guaranteed local trade properties a uniform choice cannot ensure; (2) the RODL NIBBLE (CAND-A2) - a random greedy phase builds most of the design; (3) ABSORPTION - the template absorbs the t-sets left uncovered by the nibble, finishing the exact structure.
