# H13 - Ajtai-Chvatal-Newborn-Szemeredi (1982): Crossing-free subgraphs

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): M. Ajtai, V. Chvatal, M. M. Newborn, E. Szemeredi, "Crossing-free subgraphs", in: A. Rosa, G. Sabidussi, J. Turgeon (eds.), Theory and Practice of Combinatorics (a collection of articles honoring Anton Kotzig on the occasion of his sixtieth birthday), North-Holland Mathematics Studies 60 / Annals of Discrete Mathematics 12, North-Holland, Amsterdam, 1982, pp. 9-12. Sources: Semantic Scholar (NH Math Studies 60), three SpringerLink reference lists (all with pp. 9-12 and the NHMS 60/ADM 12 identification), ouci record (editors and Kotzig dedication). Independent discovery by T. Leighton (1982) noted.
- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the crossing lemma's first proof: a graph with sufficiently many edges (at least a constant times n) has crossing number at least a constant times e^3/n^2. The lemma converts the planarity obstruction into a quantitative counting engine - the load-bearing tool for the whole of graph drawing and discrete geometry.
- Decisive moves: (1) [P10] averaging: if the crossing number is too small, the average number of crossings per edge is small; (2) [P4] iterative deletion: remove crossing-poor edges; what survives is planar but still has more than 3n-6 edges - contradiction; (3) [P9] pigeonhole inside the deletion loop. Expected P10: HIT. Batch-lineage note: the crossing lemma is the engine of Szekeley's 1997 proof of Szemeredi-Trotter (H12) - the H12/H13 pair closes the batch with a two-way engine story.
