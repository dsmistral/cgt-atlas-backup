- Summary (flagged sketch, the theorem's standard accounts): the four-functions theorem: if nonnegative functions f1, f2, f3, f4 on the subsets of an n-set satisfy f1(A)f2(B) <= f3(A u B)f4(A n B) for all A, B (log-supermodularity), then for any two set families F1, F2 the product of the f1-sums over F1 and the f2-sums over F2 is at most the product of the f3-sums over the unions family and the f4-sums over the intersections family.
