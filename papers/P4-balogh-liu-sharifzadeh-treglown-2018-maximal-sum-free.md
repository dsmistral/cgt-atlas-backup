- Summary (flagged sketch, abstract + arXiv level): the Cameron-Erdos maximal question closed sharply - for each 1 <= i <= 4 there is a constant C_i such that {1,...,n} contains (C_i + o(1))2^{n/4} maximal sum-free sets whenever n = i (mod 4) (the Cameron-Erdos lower bound: 2^{floor(n/4)}). Engines, the abstract's own list: the container and removal lemmas of Green, the Deshouillers-Freiman-Sos-Temkin structural result on sum-free sets, and the Green-Morris bound on the number of subsets of integers with small sumset. The pair's division of labor: the companion (PAMS 2015) proved fmax(n) = 2^{(1/4+o(1))n}; this paper sharpens to the constant-per-residue-class form, "substantially more involved" with "a number of new ideas" (the open Eurocomb conference version's own framing).
