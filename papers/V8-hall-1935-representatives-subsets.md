- Summary (flagged sketch, the Cameron survey record and the matching-theory histories): the marriage theorem's origin, in four and a half pages. Hall proves the necessary-and-sufficient condition for a family of subsets to admit a system of distinct representatives: every k of the subsets together contain at least k elements (the union condition) - stated in the general set-system frame (the paper's own generality, per the standard histories).
