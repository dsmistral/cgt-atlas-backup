# C3 - Tutte (1963): A census of planar maps

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): W. T. Tutte, "A Census of Planar Maps", Canadian Journal of Mathematics 15 (1963): 249-271, DOI 10.4153/CJM-1963-029-x. Open PDF located: sites.math.rutgers.edu/~zeilberger/akherim/tutte1962.pdf. Fourth paper of Tutte's map-census series.
- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): exact enumeration of rooted planar maps by edge number - the count grows like n^{-5/2} gamma^n with gamma = 27/4. Rooting removes map automorphisms; the resulting functional equations are solved by Tutte's quadratic method.
