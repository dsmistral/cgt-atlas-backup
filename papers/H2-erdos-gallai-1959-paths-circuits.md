- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the path/circuit extremal pair: an n-vertex graph with no path of more than k edges has at most kn/2 edges, with the companion bound for graphs with no long cycles; and the circumference theorem - every 2-connected graph contains a cycle of length at least min(n, 2 delta). These are the proto-results of the Erdos-Gallai school of degree-forces-structure theorems.
- Decisive moves: (1) [P8] extremal argument on maximal examples: analyze the structure forced by forbidding long paths/cycles; (2) [P4] induction on the number of vertices; (3) [P25] block decomposition: paths and cycles decompose over 2-connected blocks, reducing the problem to block pieces where min-degree forces long cycles.
