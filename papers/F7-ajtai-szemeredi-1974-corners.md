# F7 - Ajtai-Szemeredi (1974): Sets of lattice points that form no squares

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): M. Ajtai, E. Szemeredi, "Sets of lattice points that form no squares", Studia Scientiarum Mathematicarum Hungaricae 9 (1974): 9-11. TITLE AND VENUE RESOLVED vs the seed's "(verify)": the corners theorem lives in this paper. Sources: Wikipedia (Corners theorem).
- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the corners theorem - every subset of [N]^2 of sufficiently large density contains a corner {(x,y), (x+d,y), (x,y+d)}, d > 0; equivalently, a lattice set with no such right-angled isosceles pattern cannot have positive density. The proof bootstraps Szemeredi's theorem: find many points on a line, use the 1D density theorem along it, and run a density increment on the grid.
