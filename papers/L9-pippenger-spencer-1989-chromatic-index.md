- Abstract or faithful summary (summarized, flagged sketch): for k-uniform hypergraphs that are nearly regular (minimum degree asymptotic to the maximum degree) and have small codegrees (any two vertices contained together in o(D) edges), the chromatic index is asymptotic to the degree D: the edge set partitions into D(1+o(1)) matchings. The theorem strengthens and generalizes the Frankl-Rodl almost-perfect packing result (per the IBM Research record) and resolves, e.g., the chromatic index of Steiner triple systems asymptotically.
