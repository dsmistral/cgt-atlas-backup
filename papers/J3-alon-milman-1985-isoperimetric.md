- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the smallest positive eigenvalue lambda_1 of the graph's operator controls isoperimetry: vertex-expansion bounds in terms of lambda_1 and degree, so graphs with a large eigenvalue gap are expanders. Combined with results on group representations, the paper constructs many new explicit linear-sized expanders and superconcentrators.
