# K2 - Harper (1966): Optimal numberings and isoperimetric problems on graphs

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): L. H. Harper, "Optimal numberings and isoperimetric problems on graphs", J. Combin. Theory 1 (1966), DOI 10.1016/S0021-9800(66)80059-5. Sources: ScienceDirect article page (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021980066800595); secondary literature.
- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): solves the vertex-isoperimetric problem on the n-cube: among all subsets of a given size, initial segments of the simplicial order minimize the vertex boundary; the companion numbering results give optimal vertex numberings for embedding-type questions. The paper is also one of the independent discoveries of the Kruskal-Katona shadow theorem.
