# A2 - Erdos-Renyi (1959): On random graphs I

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): P. Erdos, A. Renyi, Publ. Math. Debrecen 6(3-4): 290-297, 1959, DOI 10.5486/PMD.1959.6.3-4.12. Sources: Wikipedia Erdos-Renyi model; Springer chapter on origins of random graph theory.
- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): introduces the G(n,M) random graph model and finds the connectivity threshold - the probability of connectedness jumps from near 0 to near 1 in a sharp window around M ~ (n/2) log n; analysis by counting isolated vertices and expectation.
- Decisive moves: (1) [P12] random construction as an object of study, not just a proof device - the random graph itself is the subject; (2) [P10] expectation/counting of bad events (isolated vertices, small components); (3) [CAND-A3] threshold behavior - properties flip in narrow windows; the window, not the point, is the theorem.
- Historical note (verified): the G(n,p) model was introduced independently by Gilbert (1959); G(n,M) is the Erdos-Renyi one.
