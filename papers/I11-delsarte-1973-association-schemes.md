# I11 - Delsarte (1973): An algebraic approach to the association schemes of coding theory

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): P. Delsarte, "An algebraic approach to the association schemes of coding theory", Philips Research Reports Supplement no. 10 (1973). Sources: scanned open copy at the pearl-hifi Philips archive (https://pearl-hifi.com/06_Lit_Archive/02_PEARL_Arch/Vol_16/Sec_53/Philips_Rsrch_Report_Supps_1961_thru_1976/Philips%20Research%20Reports%20Supplements%201973-10.pdf); a secondary scanned copy at scispace.
- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): founds the algebraic theory of association schemes for coding theory: codes and designs live inside the Bose-Mesner algebra of a scheme, and the linear programming (Delsarte) bound bounds maximum code size by an LP over the scheme's eigenvalue matrices, with the MacWilliams-type transform linking primal and dual. The framework yields the strongest general bounds for codes, orthogonal arrays, and designs in classical schemes (Hamming, Johnson).
