# B7 - Balogh-Morris-Samotij (2015): Independent sets in hypergraphs

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): J. Balogh, R. Morris, W. Samotij, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 28(3): 669-709, July 2015; arXiv:1204.6530 (2012). Sources: JSTOR; Illinois Experts; arXiv abstract.
- Summary (arXiv abstract, quoted-level): many important theorems (Szemeredi's theorem, the Erdos-Stone theorem) can be phrased as statements about independent sets in uniform hypergraphs; the paper proves a general theorem controlling the number and structure of independent sets in r+1-uniform hypergraphs in which every r-set is contained in at most d edges, with applications to sparse random analogues; similar results were discovered independently by Saxton and Thomason.
- Decisive moves: (1) [CAND-B5] hypergraph containers - every independent set lives in one of few small containers, so counting independent sets reduces to counting containers; (2) [P25] reduction - classical theorems rephrased as hypergraph independence statements, then transferred to the sparse random setting.
