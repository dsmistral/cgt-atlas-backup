- Abstract or faithful summary (summarized, flagged sketch; from the Project Euclid abstract): asymptotic extremal combinatorics restated model-theoretically - for finite models M, N of a universal theory without constants and function symbols (graphs, digraphs, hypergraphs), let p(M,N) be the probability that a randomly chosen |M|-element submodel of N is isomorphic to M. Which asymptotic relations exist among the densities p(M1,N),...,p(Mh,N) as |N| grows to infinity? The paper develops the calculus of FLAG ALGEBRAS: formal linear combinations of "flags" (models with labeled vertices of a fixed type) form a graded algebra; downward maps average over labelings; asymptotic relations become semialgebraic constraints on homomorphisms from the algebra to the reals; positivity questions reduce to sum-of-squares decompositions found via semidefinite programming. The first application is a new simple proof of Fisher's result on the minimal possible density of triangles in a graph with given edge density.
