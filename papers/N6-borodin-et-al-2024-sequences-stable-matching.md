- Abstract (quoted from the published JIS PDF): "We begin this paper by discussing different types of preference profiles related to the stable marriage problem. We then introduce the concept of soulmates, where a man and a woman rank each other first, and hell-pairs, where a man and a woman rank each other last. We generate sequences enumerating preference profiles of different types. We also calculate sequences related to the egalitarian cost, or 'quality', of a matching. In total, we introduce and discuss 30 new sequences related to the stable marriage problem and discuss 6 sequences that are already in the On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences."
- The decisive moves (flagged sketch; verified against the published JIS PDF):
  1. Inclusion-exclusion for exact soulmate counts: S(k,n) (Lemma 4) and the number of profiles with exactly k soulmate pairs F(k,n) (Theorem 6) via the alternating sum over forbidden extra soulmate pairs; the Latin-square variant (Theorem 9) repeats the inclusion-exclusion interior. [P3]
  2. Staged product counting: choose the k soulmate pairs (binomial), match them (k!), complete the soulmate profiles (((n-1)!)^2k) (Theorems 6, 8). [P2]
  3. The disjoint-profiles count: at most (n^2)! (each profile is a distribution of rankings among pairs - an encoding bound) and divisible by (n!)^2 (each disjoint profile corresponds to (n!)^2 profiles via the relabeling symmetry, exploited to speed the brute-force program) - Proposition 3; sequence A345679 computed to n = 4. [P2][P41]
  4. The joint-profiles count = A002860(n) x n! (Latin squares x key functions): a joint profile is uniquely defined by the women's Latin square and the key permutation - count by 1-1 correspondence. [P1]

Gale-Shapley round analysis: exactly n rounds under shared preferences (Proposition 1); one round iff each woman is exactly one man's first choice (Proposition 2); n-1 soulmate pairs force a unique stable matching reached in j rounds (Proposition 7) - the round-by-round trace is the proof, the algorithm the object of study. [P18]
