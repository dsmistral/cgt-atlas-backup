# E4 - Tutte (1954): A contribution to the theory of chromatic polynomials

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): W. T. Tutte, "A contribution to the theory of chromatic polynomials", Canadian Journal of Mathematics 6 (1954): 80-91, DOI 10.4153/CJM-1954-010-9. Sources: Cambridge Core; the Aequationes Mathematicae Tutte-polynomial survey reference list.
- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): the paper that makes the chromatic polynomial a two-way street: for a plane graph, the chromatic polynomial of the primal equals the flow polynomial of the dual - vertex-coloring and edge-flowing are one theory under planar duality. Tutte states the flow conjectures (5-flow included) here, viewing them as the dual-to-coloring problem that extends the four-colour problem beyond the plane.
