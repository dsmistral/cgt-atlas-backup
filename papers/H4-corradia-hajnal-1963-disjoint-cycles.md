# H4 - Corradi-Hajnal (1963): On the maximal number of independent circuits in a graph

- Metadata (web-verified 2026-10-13): K. Corradi and A. Hajnal, "On the maximal number of independent circuits in a graph", Acta Math. Acad. Sci. Hungar. 14: 423-439 (1963). Sources: SpringerLink (Acta Math. Acad. Sci. Hungar. 14, 423-439 (1963)), Semantic Scholar, hanspub reference record, ResearchGate (September 1963).
- Summary (general-knowledge level, flagged sketch): minimum degree at least 2k (with at least 3k vertices) forces k vertex-disjoint cycles. The theorem became the model for minimum-degree-forces-embedding statements and the standard route into cycle-packing problems.
- Decisive moves: (1) [P8] extremal/maximality analysis: consider a maximal family of disjoint cycles and analyze how the remaining vertices attach; shortest-cycle choices make the case analysis work; (2) [P4] induction on the cycle count with vertex swapping between cycles; (3) [P9] degree bookkeeping/pigeonhole: leftover vertices have enough degree to reach into the cycle family and create a better packing.
