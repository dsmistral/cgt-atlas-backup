- Summary (flagged sketch, the Redfield-rediscovery literature and the Polya-theorem histories): the symmetry-averaging engine's true origin, published a decade before Polya. Redfield counts configurations that a group identifies by reducing over the group's action - the "group-reduced distribution" machinery: objects (he works with graphs on a common vertex set) reduced by a permutation group, the counts delivered through cycle-structure bookkeeping that anticipates the cycle index.
