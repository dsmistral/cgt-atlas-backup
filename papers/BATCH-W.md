### Engine contrast and lineage (sub-chunk 25a)

- THE ALGORITHMIC TRIO'S ORIGIN ERA: W1 deferred acceptance (1962) and W3 the cut tree (1961) predate the polyhedral turn (W12 Edmonds at 25b closes the arc) - both prove existence by explicit terminating procedure, the P18 canon before algorithmic proof was a method name; contrast: W1 terminates by strict-preference descent (finite lists, at most n^2 proposals), W3 by the non-crossing-cuts induction on vertex sets.
- THE SPECTRAL LINE: W2 (1960) the adjacency-matrix eigenvalue classification -> the J batch's P21 program (J1 Hoffman's chromatic bound, J2 Lovasz theta, J3 Alon-Milman) - the origin-era firing a full decade before the program it founded; the d=57 Moore graph the field's open extremal question since.
- THE RANK LINE: W4 (1971) the address-matrix rank lower bound -> the I batch's polynomial-independence firings (I3 Ray-Chaudhuri-Wilson, I4 Frankl-Wilson) - the linear-algebra lower-bound tradition from its Bell Labs era (the squashed-cube companion 1972; the addressing target the loop-switching network).
- THE PATH-RAMSEY LINE: W5 (1967) the exact values R(P_k, P_l) -> Gyarfas-Lehel 1973 and Faudree-Schelp 1975 (the bipartite and general forms) -> the Erdos-Gyarfas 1995 path-cover conjecture -> its recent resolution (Pokrovskiy-Versteegen-Williams) - the F-batch extension's origin-to-frontier arc.
- THE POSET PAIR COMPLETES: V11 Dilworth (max antichain = min chain cover) and W6 Mirsky (min antichain cover = max chain) - the dual pair both gathered at their origins; the engines contrast honestly: the width theorem generated a short-proofs literature (the LP and Konig-based routes), the dual is the easy side (the height levels), and the paper's own contribution is the compactness extension.
- THE TERMINATION COUNTS: W1's at most n^2 proposals (no man proposes twice to the same woman) and W3's n-1 flow computations (each condensation step merges the vertex count) - the two P18 classics' termination arguments are both pigeonhole counts at heart (the P9 cross-note, no census rows).
