- Abstract or faithful summary (summarized, flagged sketch): the trilogy proves Wilson's existence theorem: for any fixed set K of block sizes at least 2, a pairwise balanced design PBD(v, K) exists for all sufficiently large v satisfying the necessary divisibility (admissibility) conditions forced by pairwise counting - asymptotically, the divisibility conditions are the ONLY obstructions to existence. Part I builds the composition machinery (Wilson's fundamental construction: new PBDs assembled from ingredient designs on transversal-design templates; morphisms between PBD-closed sets). Part II proves PBD-closed sets of integers are finitely generated (eventual periodicity - an elementary number-theory result in the Frobenius-coin tradition). Part III combines the two into the proof of the existence conjecture, completing the program Hanani began (L3) and settling the general asymptotic existence question for designs.
