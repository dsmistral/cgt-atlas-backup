## Standing goal

The project's ultimate goal is **full coverage of the load-bearing canon**: every important solved problem family of combinatorics & graph theory, and every repeatable solution pattern, properly arranged into chapters (the atlas's Purpose section states this; its own honest caveat is that the *canon*, not "all problems ever published", is the target).
